Peakaboo

Point your phone at the mountains — they introduce themselves.

Peakaboo labels the summits you can actually see on a live camera view: name, height, and distance, with real terrain line-of-sight. Fully offline once your area is loaded. No account, no subscription.

Support: email rdunsmore@pm.me — bug reports and peak-name corrections welcome (peak data comes from OpenStreetMap, so fixes help everyone).
