Peakaboo Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-07

Peakaboo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Location

Your location is used on your device to determine which mountain summits are around you. It never leaves your phone.

Camera

The camera feed is displayed live so peak labels can be drawn over it. It is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Network use

Peakaboo downloads publicly available map data (peak names from OpenStreetMap) and elevation data (Terrain Tiles on AWS) for your general area. These requests are ordinary anonymous file downloads and contain no personal information. Downloaded data is cached on your device so the app works offline.
